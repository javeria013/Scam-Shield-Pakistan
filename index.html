        Common scams in Pakistan

        🏦

        Fake bank messages

        "Your account will be blocked." They send a link and ask for your PIN or OTP. Banks never ask for these.

        📱

        Easypaisa / JazzCash

        "Money was sent by mistake, please return it." The first transfer is fake or the caller wants your wallet PIN.

        📦

        Courier and parcel

        "Pay a small redelivery fee." The link steals your card details. Check the parcel in the official courier app.

        🎁

        Prize and BISP messages

        "You won Rs 25,000!" Real prizes never ask for a fee, tax or CNIC details first.

        💼

        Fake online jobs

        "Earn Rs 5,000 daily from home." They ask for a registration fee, then disappear.

        🔑

        OTP requests

        A caller says they are from your bank and asks for the code you just received. That code gives them access.

        Safety tips

        • Never share your OTP, PIN or password. No bank, wallet or company needs it from you.
        • Do not click links in unexpected messages. Open the official app or type the website yourself.
        • Stop when you feel rushed. Scammers use fear and short deadlines to stop you thinking.
        • Real prizes and jobs are free. If someone asks for a fee first, it is a scam.
        • Call the official number. Use the number on the back of your card or the company's website, not the one in the message.
        • Talk to someone. Show the message to family or friends before you act.

        Report a scam

        If you lost money, contact your bank or wallet company first, then report it. Contact details can change, so confirm them on the official websites.

        Cybercrime (NCCIA)

        The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency now handles online fraud complaints. Report online at nccia.gov.pk or call the cybercrime helpline 1799.

        Spam and fraud SMS (PTA)

        Forward the spam SMS to 9000. Or file a complaint at complaint.pta.gov.pk or call 0800-55055.

        Your bank or wallet

        Call the helpline printed on your card or in the official app. Ask them to block the account or card quickly.

        WhatsApp and social media

        Use the Report and Block options inside the app on the scam number or profile.

        About this project

        Scam Shield Pakistan is a student AI project. It uses two checks together.

        1. Rule-based check

        The browser looks for known scam words and risky links, in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu. It is fast and works without AI.

        2. AI check

        The message is sent to a secure server function. It asks Google Gemini to judge the message, and the secret API key never reaches your browser.

        The final result combines both. If the rules find more danger than the AI, the risk level is raised by one step.

        Please note: This tool can make mistakes. It is a helper, not a replacement for your own judgment, your bank, or the police. Technology used: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Vercel serverless functions and the Gemini API.
